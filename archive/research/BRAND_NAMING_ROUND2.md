Date: 2026-10-02. Nothing was renamed, registered or bought. This is **preliminary naming
research, not legal trademark clearance.** Store checks are a Chrome Web Store search for the
bare name; domain checks are registry (RDAP) lookups; trademark notes come from public
listings found by web search, not from a search of USPTO, EUIPO or TÜRKPATENT registers.

## Eliminated, with the reason

| Reason | Names |
|---|---|
| Same-name or near-name new tab / homepage extension | Alcove (“Alcove – Custom New Tab”), Halcyon, Scenery (“Scenery Tab” and many themes), Hearthside and Hearthly (“Hearth” new tabs), Prelude (a new-tab page under the Chinese word for prelude, plus “Prelude”) |
| Same-name extension in another category | Evenfall (AI chat and notes), Greenroom, Vesper (four), Nocturne (seven), Islet, Aerie, Limen (three), Morrow, Portico (“All your tools, right where you want them”) |
| Established software or company of the same name | Outset (Outset.ai, Outset Medical marks, macadmins/outset), Backlot (YC-backed film tool), Atoll (macOS Dynamic Island app, 4.8k stars), Mirador (IIIF viewer), Entrada (Entrata; generic Spanish word), Hestia and Vesta (control panels, WordPress theme), Keylight (Foundry keyer, Elgato), Lodestar, Modus, Overture, Daybreak, Nightfall, Firstlight, Roost, Ambit, Origo, Solace, Oriel (NHS recruitment system) |
| Spelling or pronunciation fails the “heard once” test | Foreword (forward), Stead, Aerie, Orrery, Vignette, Limen, Gloaming (kept only as a wildcard) |
| Meaning works against the product | Haunt, Onset (medical), Homing (generic), Mooring |
| Coined but empty: sounds generated, evokes nothing | Orvane, Velmo, Nuvara, Domira, Calmora, Portara, Ondra, Avelo, Aviro, Oreon, Solvea, Halden, Talvi, Elvio, Mireo, Casana, Verdan, Stratia, Realma, Ambrel, Eloria, Miravo, Lumora, Arrivo, Weven, Scenea, Limina, Senda, Homara |
| Too literal / utility-sounding | Daydeck, Openroom, Modehouse, Skydeck, Startline, Webhaven, Wayhome, Homeport, Homeward, Soundstage |
