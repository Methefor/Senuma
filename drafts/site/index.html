What Senuma does with your data: nothing.

  • Your Spaces, links and settings are stored in your browser, on your device.
  • No account. No analytics. No tracking. Nothing is sent to us.
  • A search goes to your browser's default search engine, or to the provider you pick for it.
  • Icons come from each site itself by default, so no one else learns which sites you saved.
  • Bookmarks and recently closed pages are read only if you turn those features on.
