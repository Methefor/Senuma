**Senuma privacy policy**

Last updated: [date of publication]

Senuma (previously New Tab Folders) is a new tab page for Chrome. It is built to work on your
device, without an account.

**What Senuma stores, and where**

Your Spaces, links, Modes, settings and restore points are stored in your browser, on your
device. Images you add as wallpapers are stored on your device and are not included in backup
files. None of this is sent to us.

**What Senuma does not do**

- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
- No account and no sign-in.
- No reading of the pages you visit; Senuma does not run on websites.
- No location.
- No selling or sharing of data. We do not receive your data in the first place.

**Optional permissions**

Senuma asks for these only if you turn the feature on, and you can turn them off again:

- *Bookmarks* — only when you choose to import your browser bookmarks. They are read once,
  never changed, and the permission is given back straight after.
- *Tabs and sessions* — only when you turn on recently closed pages, so they can be listed and
  reopened. They are read on your device and never stored or sent.

**Requests your browser makes**

- Searches go to your browser's default search engine, or to a provider you pick.
- To show an icon for a saved link, your browser may request that site's own icon. If you choose
  the optional icon service, Google receives the site names of your saved links.

**If you used cloud sync in New Tab Folders 1.x**

Senuma has no cloud sync and does not contact the service that version 1.x used. Your setup on
this device was converted locally. Data that version 1.x saved to the cloud (your folders, links
and settings, linked to your Google account's e-mail address and name) [is deleted on <date> /
remains stored until <date>]. To have it deleted sooner, write to [contact e-mail].

**Removing your data**

Removing the extension removes everything Senuma stored on your device. You can export a backup
file first from Settings.

**Changes**

If Senuma ever starts to collect anything, this policy and the store listing will be updated
first, and it will be opt-in.
