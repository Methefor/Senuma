## 5. Verdict

Avluna is a credible fallback. Its trademark picture is lighter than Senuma's (no registered
near-identical mark in classes 9/42; the closest is a pending US application in another
field), but it carries more search and domain baggage: the Avaluna apps, the adult-spam
`.com`, and the busy “avlu” root in Turkey.
